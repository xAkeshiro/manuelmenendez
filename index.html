He arrived in China in 1979 carrying a simple belief: nations that trade together grow closer together.

Manuel “Manny” Menendez III landed in the opening months of reform and opening up. As chief executive of Great Eastern Development, he helped assemble the first U.S. and China equity joint venture: a partnership between CITIC and Beatrice, the Chicago based Fortune 500 food company, that built the Guangmei Foods factory in southern China. It is credited as the first joint venture in the country with a Fortune 500 partner.

He learned China from its reform era architects, working with figures such as Gu Mu, Chen Muhua, Wan Li and Rong Yiren, and meeting a young Jiang Zemin, then Party Secretary of Shanghai, at the Peace Hotel. The ventures that followed reached from food production to bicycle manufacturing with Shanghai’s storied Forever and Phoenix marques. In 1985, Deng Xiaoping gave him the Chinese name by which Beijing still introduces him: Meng Deshi, 孟德士. Of everything he has witnessed since, he says China’s growth is “not good, it’s been a miracle.”

His career has also run through public life. From 2001 to 2004 he served as executive director of the Office of Economic Development for the City and County of Honolulu under Mayor Jeremy Harris, helping launch the Sunset on the Beach festival and develop the Waipio Peninsula Soccer Park. He has paired enterprise with civic service in Hawaii ever since, including the board of the Pacific War Memorial Association. Today, as founder, chairman and CEO of MCM Group Holdings, he remains one of the longest serving American voices on trans-Pacific trade, from Tsinghua University lecture halls to the China and Globalization Forum, the China International Supply Chain Expo and Harvard’s China forum, carrying one consistent message: more dialogue, more trust, more trade.

Guangmei Foods

1985

Named by Deng Xiaoping

The architect of reform gives him his Chinese name, 孟德士. In the same era he builds bicycle ventures with the Forever 永久 and Phoenix 凤凰 brands.

1988

Founds MCM Group

Establishes the firm he still leads as founder, chairman and chief executive, advising and investing across U.S. and China markets.

2001

Public service in Honolulu

Pacific War Memorial Association
